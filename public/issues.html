Q: How do I manage issues?
A: Use the Triage board to move the status of a ticket. Note that closing an issue requires valid resolution remarks for the citizen.

Q: Where can I see metrics?
A: The Overview tab automatically calculates resolution times and tracks closed tickets.

Q: Can I export data?
A: Yes, click 'Export CSV' to download a spreadsheet of all reports including categories, urgencies, and user ratings.
